At a glance

Blotter: Message Filter is an iPhone app developed by {{developer}}. It filters eligible texts from unknown senders using keywords you choose. It requires no account and uses no advertising, tracking, or third-party analytics SDKs.

On-device message processing

When you enable Blotter in iPhone Settings, iOS supplies eligible incoming SMS/MMS messages to its filter extension. The filter compares the message text with your saved keywords and matching options on your iPhone, then returns a classification to iOS.

Message text is not sent to the developer or a developer-operated server. The filter does not use the sender field. Blotter does not save message bodies, senders, message timestamps, filter decisions, or a history of keyword matches.

Information stored on your iPhone

Blotter stores your configured keywords, matching options, and setup status in shared app preferences so the app and its filter can use your rules. These preferences may be included in device backups according to your Apple settings.

The developer does not receive your rules or message data from the app. They are not sold or shared with advertising or analytics services.

Retention, deletion, and disabling the filter

Keyword rules stay in app storage until you remove them. You can stop future filtering by deselecting Blotter in the text message filtering settings under iPhone Settings → Apps → Messages.

Using Delete App removes local app storage; offloading preserves app data. The app and its filter share the current Blotter app group for rules and matching options, not message history. Blotter stores no activity history or message data in Keychain. Device backups are managed separately through Apple’s controls. Blotter does not control messages retained in Apple’s Messages app.
