At a glance

Day in Ink: Habit Tracker is an iPhone app developed by {{developer}}. It works without an account. Your habit data is stored on your device and is not sent to the developer. The app has no advertising, tracking, or third-party analytics SDKs.

Information stored on your iPhone

The app stores your habit names, emoji, colors, tracking settings, daily defaults, goals, steps, units, creation dates, start days, archive status, and dated check-ins and progress. Start days and recorded days use stable Gregorian civil day keys; goals, steps, and recorded amounts use decimal values. Recorded days can include the tracking type, goal, and unit that applied at the time.

This information is used to display your habits, calculate progress and trends, and restore your records when you reopen the app. It is not sold or shared with advertising or analytics services.

Device backups and exported files

App data may be included in device backups according to your device and Apple account settings. Those backups are managed through Apple’s controls and services.

You can export a version 3 JSON backup and import one using Files. Only version 3 backups are supported. Exported files contain your habit names, settings, and recorded history. Day in Ink does not encrypt exported files. The storage provider you choose may store or sync them under its own settings and privacy policy.

Importing replaces your current habits and history after confirmation. Deleting information in the app does not delete exported copies or earlier device backups.

Retention and deletion

Your habits and check-ins remain in app storage until you delete them. Archiving a habit keeps its history. Permanently deleting a habit removes it and its check-ins from current app storage.

Using Delete App removes local app storage; offloading the app preserves its data. Manage device backups and exported files separately. The developer cannot access or delete habit data stored only on your device or in your own backups.
